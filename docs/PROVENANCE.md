# Provenance, contribution and sharing

## Editorial scope

This repository was prepared from a privately held Chinese-language project archive. New explanatory documents are source-grounded English summaries, not a complete certified translation of the original reports.

Source labels and SHA-256 fingerprints are recorded in [`source-manifest.json`](../source-manifest.json). Original local paths are kept outside the repository. The public artifact register distinguishes direct copies, package-level image extracts, cached-chart transcriptions and generated figures.

## Sharing and attribution

The original research was a team effort. Repository maintenance by Jingfan Xin does not imply sole ownership of questionnaires, team reports, participant data or third-party publications. No blanket open-source licence is assigned to those materials.

The public repository contains only curated documentation, aggregate outputs, non-identifying embedded figures and the plain-text S05 result log. Original reports, respondent-level data, interview materials, the S06 workbook and the private source-path index remain excluded. Public access does not grant redistribution rights to excluded materials.
